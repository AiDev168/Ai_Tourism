## Stage 1 limitations

- ZIP identity matching is deterministic exact-key/name plus conservative review leads; not an editorial or field-level source audit.
- Similarity/coordinate proximity only generates possible-match review rows; it does not merge records.
- Source URLs not found in current DB are only **candidate new references**. They are not verified distinct publishers or approved sources.
- Uploaded document/chunk records are not treated as accepted or semantically valid. Embedding presence is an aggregate count; it is not relevance evidence.
